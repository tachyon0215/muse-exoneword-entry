# muse-exoneword-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [Describe Your Ex in One Word](https://make.fun/bounty/u-puan-describe-your-ex-in-one-word-9fc6ebe7).

## The entry

> Nonrefundable

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: 'Lesson' is a plain cliche and 'Expensive' is a plain cost insult; 'Nonrefundable' wins because it is one correctly spelled word with a twist - the time is spent and none of it comes back - instantly readable as a verdict on a former partner, with recognition and no caption needed.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 e6ae3e37dc6a2287bb64213e1d70554629dbd06cd3c41fcf1ebbf84449ef17dc).
